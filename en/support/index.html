How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Easeoff. In the app, My plan → Manage subscription goes to the same place.

I changed phones and my subscription is missing

Tap My plan → Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Do my logs move to a new phone?

Your logs live only on your phone. Restoring your new iPhone from a backup of the old one brings them along.
